Our Story & Evolution

From Humble Beginnings to a World-Class University

Haridwar University was conceived with a bold vision: to establish an institution of global distinction on the fertile banks of the Ganga Canal in Roorkee—a historic town internationally renowned as an educational and scientific hub.

Originating as the prestigious Roorkee College of Engineering (RCE), the institution set unprecedented standards in technical pedagogy and research. Recognising its continuous academic elevation, the Government of Uttarakhand legislated its transition into a full-fledged University under the Haridwar University Act.

Today, across 20+ acres of self-contained, Wi-Fi enabled green grounds, Haridwar University accommodates five autonomous constituent colleges, dozens of advanced laboratories, and an inspiring community of 10,000+ students and distinguished faculty scholars.
