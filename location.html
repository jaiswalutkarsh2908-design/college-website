Multi-Modal Connectivity

🚂
By Railway:

Nearest Railway Station: Roorkee (RK) - 5 km. Connected via Delhi-Dehradun Shatabdi Express, Jan Shatabdi, and regular express trains from all major Indian metros.

🚗
By Road & Highway:

Directly on the Roorkee–Haridwar Canal Road. 175 km from New Delhi via the Delhi-Meerut Expressway & NH-58. Frequent deluxe state & private buses ply 24/7.

✈️
By Airway:

Nearest Airport: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (DED) - ~60 km. Daily domestic flights from Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Kolkata.
