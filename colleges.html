Our Five Constituent Colleges

Specialized schools of excellence offering multidisciplinary, industry-integrated degree programs engineered for top careers.

💻
AI, Data & Software

1. Roorkee College of Smart Computing

At the forefront of computing technologies, this college specializes in emerging technologies including Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Cloud Computing, Big Data, and Cyber Security. Featuring modern NVIDIA supercomputing workstations and certified IBM/Wipro training labs.

Programs Offered:

B.Tech - Computer Science & Engineering (4 Years) B.Tech - Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (4 Years) B.Tech - Cyber Security & Forensic Science (4 Years) B.Tech - Cloud & Mobile Computing (4 Years) BCA - Bachelor of Computer Applications (3 Years) MCA - Master of Computer Applications (2 Years)
Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics & Mathematics (Min 50%) Enquire for Admission →
⚙️
Core Industry

2. Roorkee College of Engineering & Technology

Rooted in RCE's distinguished legacy since 2010, this college produces robust engineers across core fields. Equipped with high-capacity machine shops, wind tunnel testing apparatus, concrete testing laboratories, power systems setups, and VLSI circuit design suites.

Programs Offered:

B.Tech - Civil Engineering (4 Years) B.Tech - Mechanical Engineering (4 Years) B.Tech - Electrical & Electronics Engineering (4 Years) M.Tech - Structural Engineering / Thermal Engineering (2 Years) Polytechnic Diploma in Engineering (3 Years)
Eligibility: 10+2 with PCM (Min 50%) or Lateral Entry for Diploma Enquire for Admission →
📊
Management

3. Roorkee College of Business Studies

Nurturing future corporate leaders, entrepreneurs, and investment analysts. Featuring business simulation incubators, Bloomberg financial terminal access, live stock market trading rooms, and frequent guest sessions by senior CXOs.

Programs Offered:

MBA - Marketing, Finance, HR & International Business (2 Years) MBA - Business Analytics & Supply Chain (2 Years) BBA - Bachelor of Business Administration (3 Years) B.Com (Honours) - Accounting & Taxation (3 Years)
Eligibility: Graduation (50%) for MBA | 10+2 Any Stream for BBA/B.Com Enquire for Admission →
🌾
Agritech

4. Roorkee College of Agriculture Sciences

Strategically located in Uttarakhand's rich agricultural plain, our college blends traditional agronomy with modern precision agriculture, hydroponics, drone spraying, genetic seed testing, and agribusiness supply chain management.

Programs Offered:

B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture (4 Years) M.Sc Agronomy & Horticulture (2 Years) Certificate in Organic Farming & Seed Technology
Eligibility: 10+2 with PCB/PCM or Agriculture (Min 50%) Enquire for Admission →
🏥
Healthcare & Medicine

5. Roorkee College of Allied Sciences

Approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and State Paramedical Councils. Delivering top-tier clinical and pharmaceutical instruction with active hospital rotations, pharmacology labs, and community health outreach.

Programs Offered:

B.Pharm - Bachelor of Pharmacy (4 Years) D.Pharm - Diploma in Pharmacy (2 Years) B.Sc Nursing (4 Years) BMLT - Medical Lab Technology (3 Years)
Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Biology (Min 50%) Enquire for Admission →
