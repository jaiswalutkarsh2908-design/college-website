Legacy of Educational Excellence

A Modern University Rooted in Values & Innovation

Established in 2010 on the picturesque banks of the holy Ganga Canal in Roorkee, Haridwar University has grown from a premier engineering college into one of Uttarakhand's most distinguished universities.

Surrounded by the serene Indo-Gangetic plains, our campus provides a distraction-free, high-tech haven for over 10,000 learners. With state-of-the-art supercomputing labs, experiential agriculture research farms, and an advanced healthcare training center, we bridge academic theory with real-world corporate demands.
